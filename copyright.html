Privacy and User-Submitted Information

Information Collected

Certain games and applications request information such as real names, codenames, phone numbers, hall or group information, and other participant details. Contact form submissions may include your name, email address, subject, and message. Some applications may also store a game identifier in your browser to recognize your participation.

Purpose of Collection

Submitted information is used to register participants, manage games, maintain records, support game administration, and facilitate game-related communications where applicable. Contact messages are used to respond to inquiries.

Storage and Access

Depending on the game, submissions are processed through Supabase or Google Apps Script and the storage configured for that application. Contact form submissions are processed through Google Apps Script and sent by email to the developer. Depending on the game's features, administrators and other participants may see certain game-related details. Do not submit information you are unwilling to share for participation.

Information is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated advertising. Information is processed by service providers necessary to operate the applications, including Supabase and Google services where applicable. Game information may remain stored until it is deleted or reset by an administrator. A game reset may not remove all records or backups. Contact messages may remain in the receiving email account.
